**Customs Copilot is an independent, unofficial project.** It is not a product of, and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR), Pakistan Customs, the Pakistan Single Window or the Government of Pakistan. It uses no government logo, emblem or branding.

**Not advice.** Everything the software returns (tariff codes, duty and tax amounts, notes, citations) is decision support computed from publicly available documents. It is not legal, tax or customs advice. Classification, valuation and the duty payable are decided by Pakistan Customs at assessment, and rules change during the year (for example by notifications that may not be in this tool). Check with Pakistan Customs or a licensed customs agent before you rely on a result.

**Accuracy.** The documents are read from scans and PDFs by software and some rows are flagged for human review. The measured accuracy is published in the repository, with its limits; it is not 100%. The software says "I'm not sure" when it cannot reach a code it trusts, and it refuses requests it cannot compute (for example mobile phones and electric vehicles).

**Sources.** The tariff, SROs, Acts, rulings and public notices are publications of the Government of Pakistan and remain subject to their own terms. This repository does not redistribute them; see `docs/sources.md` for where to obtain them.
